Work With Integro

To design & implement reclamation
of mined-out landscapes

Mining Lessees and PSUs

Bring closure into the operating plan from day one — a shared data record across active and abandoned holdings, a single integrated team instead of a chain of contractors, and a monitoring layer that serves both reclamation and DGMS safety compliance at once.

Government and Policy Bodies

A documented, data-backed path from fees and taxes to measurable climate, biodiversity and public-health outcomes — built to sit alongside existing national efforts on scientific mine closure, rather than propose a competing framework.

Research and Academic Institutions

Field-generated data — like our Beema Bamboo finding from Gomtu — offered as a basis for joint research, validation, or a pilot study, not as a finished conclusion looking for endorsement.
