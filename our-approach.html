Our grand objective

A dedicated, Exclusive
Mine Reclamation Organisation

Integro Mine Reclamation exists to do one thing: plan,
design and execute mine reclamation, PAN India.

Mining remains a significant contributor to the world
economy; Integro exists to repair and lessen its impact,
and restore mined-out land to usability.

Integro Mine Reclamation Pvt. Ltd. builds the singular driving force behind circular mine closure — reclamation, remediation and progressive rehabilitation, run as one.
