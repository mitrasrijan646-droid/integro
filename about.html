Who We Are

What We Do

A dedicated, Exclusive
Mine Reclamation Organisation

Integro Mine Reclamation Pvt. Ltd. exists to plan, design and execute
integrated mine closure strategies.

Mining remains a significant contributor to the world economy. Integro enhances
their operations by calibrating procedures that safegruard the environment, so that
the impact of mining lessens to a great extent and restore the mined-out land to usability.

Moreover, Integro brings in techno-creative expression to the mined-out
landscapes that elevates their socio-economic value.

Our vision

To help mining leave the land fit to live on, long after extraction ends.

Two words carry the whole mandate. Repair - the land, the water, the air already affected by extraction. Restore - the site's usability, for a community, an ecosystem, or an economy, once mining is done.

How it's
usually done

A single contractor is usually brought in late, asked to execute against a checklist, with no independent view of the land and no mandate to make judgment calls beyond what the contract specifies. It's a structural gap, not a failure of effort — the model itself isn't built for judgment.

How Integro
works instead

A dedicated reclamation organisation, integrating roughly 25 disciplines — geology, hydrology, agronomy, energy, safety, community management — around one closure strategy, backed by centralised data and continuous monitoring, from the first extraction to the last.

Founder

Founded on a career spent inside the mining and industrial equipment world.

Integro Mine Reclamation Pvt. Ltd. was founded by Ambalika Dey in 2019. She has spent sixteen years working inside the mining and industrial equipment ecosystem.

At PwC — the largest of the Big Four in 2010 — she gained hands-on experience in branding and its analytical dimension, grounded in the discipline of one of the world's leading consulting firms. At CDE Global, an Irish mineral-washing equipment manufacturer, she took on branding for their Indian chapter, learning to work within dynamics entirely different from the parent company's global market.

At Tractors India, she was introduced to Caterpillar, one of the world's foremost makers of earth-moving and mining equipment — and it was here that her branding skills evolved to the point of being recognised with a Caterpillar Certified Brand Advocate distinction in 2016, one of only 110 named worldwide that year, selected through the company's own training and examination process.

When Tractors India became Gainwell Commosales, Ambalika was part of that transition, designing the new GCPL brand identity from the ground up.

That continuity carried into her most defining project, Unnati — Gainwell Commosales' integrated centre, Asia's second and the world's seventh LEED v4 Platinum-certified facility.

There, her role went beyond brand design into PMC-level coordination, working alongside architects, civil engineers and interior designers to ensure the brand was built into the building's surfaces and textures, not layered on top of them.

She also leads Erma Consulting OPC Pvt. Ltd., a brand ecosystem design firm built on the same principle Integro now applies to reclamation: coordinating specialists across disciplines toward one integrated outcome.

At Integro, Ambalika leads strategy, stakeholder relationships and system design; technical delivery runs through the company's own integrated model — 25 interconnected departments, spanning geology, hydrology, agronomy and safety engineering, bound together as one accountable system.
