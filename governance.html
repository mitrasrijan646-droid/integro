Governance & Policies

Integro Mine Reclamation
Pvt. Ltd.

Structure, oversight and operating policy — stated plainly, for readers evaluating Integro as an organisation rather than as a proposal.

For founder background and career history, see About Us.

01 Legal structure

A private limited company, incorporated in India.

Integro Mine Reclamation Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated on September 5, 2019.

Registered Office: Repose, 2nd Floor, 29/17 Kendua Main Road, Garia, Kolkata, West Bengal, India

02 Board & oversight

Standard company governance under the Companies Act.

Integro operates under standard private limited company governance as required under the Companies Act, 2013. Director details and shareholding are a matter of public record with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and are supplied directly as part of formal due-diligence processes on request — they are not published on this page.

03 · Code of conduct

Principles

Field findings and site diagnostics are reported as found — even when they run against expectations. Every one of the 25 disciplines operating under Integro's model is held to the same standard.

Conflict of Interest

Site assessments are conducted without conflicts of interest. Integro does not evaluate a site in which it, or anyone connected to it, holds an undisclosed financial interest.

Safety

Safety is never subordinated to schedule or cost, consistent with the DGMS-aligned systems described on Our Approach.

04 · Data handling and confidentiality

Integro treats site data, client information, and government-shared records as confidential by default. Findings generated for one mining lessee, PSU, or government body are never shared with another party without explicit permission — including the state-by-state mine inventory and fund figures referenced on Data & Impact, and the field diagnostics referenced on the Just Transition Case Study, both of which are held for direct sharing with mining lessees, PSUs, and government policy partners rather than published openly.

Data collected during site visits and technical studies is used solely for the purpose it was gathered for, and retained only as long as it serves an active engagement or a partner's ongoing reference needs.

05 · Regulatory alignment

Built to meet, not just reference, existing regulation.

Integro's systems and advisory are built to align with Directorate General of Mines Safety requirements and applicable Indian environmental and mine closure regulation, including the framework set out under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act and the Coal Controller Organisation's mine closure guidelines. Full detail on how this operates day to day sits on Our Approach; this page confirms the commitment as a matter of governance, not just practice.

Integro Mine Reclamation Pvt. Ltd. builds the singular driving force behind circular mine closure — reclamation, remediation and progressive rehabilitation, run as one.
