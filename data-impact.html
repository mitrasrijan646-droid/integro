The Scale of What's Already Waiting to Be Reclaimed

The numbers in India

The National Picture

At the release of AAROH — the Ministry of Coal's Annual Report on Mine Closure — Prof. Pradip Swarnakar of IIT Kanpur confirmed India's progress: 42 coal mines successfully closed, with a further 147 identified for structured closure, 136 under Coal India Limited and 11 under Singareni Collieries Company Limited. The new Mine Closure Guidelines provide for an escrow fund to support community development, alongside the Coal Controller Organisation's RECLAIM framework and the L.I.V.E.S. roadmap.

Source

Ministry of Coal, Government of India, at the release of AAROH — Annual Report on Mine Closure.
